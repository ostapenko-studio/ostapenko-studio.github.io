The app

What you get

  • A clear verdict in plain words — great drying day, good time to hang, slow, risky, or better dry indoors — with the reason in one sentence.
  • The best drying window for today, down to the minute, and roughly when a full load should be dry.
  • A bring-in deadline that says why: rain on the way, or just the evening damp.
  • The next three days as laundry advice with the best washing day tagged — "wash today or Saturday?" takes one glance.
  • Quiet, optional alerts: a heads-up the evening before a good day, a nudge when the window opens, and a warning before rain reaches the line.
  • A "play it safe with rain" switch for people who'd rather rewash nothing, ever.

Honest about forecasts

A forecast is a guess, and Drying Day says so. It hedges where hedging is honest, names the day's main risk out loud, and leaves the final call where it belongs — with the sky over your own line.

Quietly respectful

No tracking. No data sales. Your location goes straight to the weather service to fetch the forecast and nowhere else — we run no servers and collect nothing.
