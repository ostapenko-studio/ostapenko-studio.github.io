What the app is — and isn't

Drying Day turns third-party weather forecast data (WeatherAPI.com) into practical laundry advice: whether outdoor drying looks worthwhile, the best stretch of the day to hang washing out, roughly how long a load should take, and when to bring it back in. Everything it shows is a reading of a forecast, not a promise about the weather. Forecasts shift, rain arrives unannounced, and microclimates are real — a balcony in the shade dries differently from a sunny yard. The final say always belongs to the sky over your own line, and decisions about your washing are yours.
