PawWalk

Is it too hot to walk the dog — and when today can we go?

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

The app

What you get

  • A straight answer on the horizon line: how hot the ground under paws is likely to be, right now, where you are.
  • Estimated surface temperatures for asphalt, concrete, and grass — asphalt is the worst case, and the app treats it that way.
  • A day-strip of walk windows: the green bars are the cooler hours, so you can plan the morning and evening walks around the scorching middle.
  • Three dog-profile toggles — puppy or senior, flat-faced, heat-sensitive coat — each nudging every verdict more careful. No breed databases, no forms.
  • A home-screen widget with the current verdict at a glance.
  • The seven-second hand test, always: back of your hand on the path — too hot for you is too hot for paws.

Built for Australia

Summer asphalt here can run 25 degrees hotter than the air and burn a paw in about a minute. PawWalk treats that as the everyday reality it is — calm, practical, and on your side, never scolding.

Quietly respectful

No tracking. No data sales. Your location goes straight to the weather service to fetch the forecast and nowhere else — we run no servers and collect nothing.

Free with a single unobtrusive banner; one small one-time purchase removes it forever. No subscriptions, ever.
