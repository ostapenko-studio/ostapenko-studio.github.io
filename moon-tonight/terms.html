What the app is — and isn't

Moon Tonight shows the Moon's phase, its illuminated fraction, moonrise and moonset times, and the dates of the next full and new Moon for your location or a place you choose. Phase, illumination and event dates are calculated on your device using standard published astronomical algorithms and are checked against published tables to within a couple of minutes; moonrise and moonset times come from a third-party provider (WeatherAPI.com). The picture of the Moon is an illustration: it shows the lit and dark portions and, in "Local sky" mode, the tilt of the lit edge for your place and time, but it does not reproduce the Moon's libration or the shadows along its edge, and the app makes no claim about whether the Moon is visible — cloud, horizon, daylight and local obstructions are outside its scope.

The app is an informational tool. It should not be used as the sole basis for decisions involving personal safety, navigation, tides, or any activity timed to the Moon. For authoritative data consult your national observatory or almanac.
