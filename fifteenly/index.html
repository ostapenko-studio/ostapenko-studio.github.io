Fifteenly

The classic 15 puzzle, made calm. Every scramble is solvable.

Get it on Google Play · Coming soon to the App Store

What you get

  • The sliding number puzzle you know: slide tiles into the empty space until the numbers are back in order. Tap a tile, or swipe on the board.
  • Every scramble is solvable — each one is checked before it reaches you, so there is never a board that cannot be finished.
  • 240 puzzles across six tiers, from a gentle 3×3 to a demanding 6×6, getting steadily harder within each tier.
  • A new daily puzzle, the same for everyone, that ramps up through the week.
  • Hints that show your next move, chosen by the real row-by-row method, and a How to play guide that teaches it in full.
  • A timer, a move counter and your best times, all kept on your device.
  • Light and dark themes, in 50 languages.

Quietly respectful

Fully offline. No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics and no leaderboards. Your progress and settings stay on your phone — we run no servers and collect nothing ourselves. Ads are never personalised.

Free to play, with a small banner and optional short videos if you would like a hint or a bonus puzzle. One inexpensive purchase removes the ads for good and makes hints and bonus puzzles free. No subscriptions, ever.
