Privacy Policy

Pairbound: Dominosa Puzzle, by Ostapenko Studio · Effective 14 November 2026

This English text is the canonical version. Translations, where provided, are for convenience; where a translation differs, this version applies to the extent local law allows.

Pairbound is a Dominosa logic puzzle: you pair up neighbouring numbers until every domino is used exactly once. It was built to collect as little data as possible. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics trackers, and we operate no servers — nothing you do in the app is sent to us. Every puzzle, including the Daily Puzzle, is generated on your device, and the game works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (the puzzles you have solved, Journey progress, statistics, streaks, the board you are working on and its undo history, and the hints you used), your settings (checking mode, domino tray, sound and haptics, timer, theme) and whether you have purchased the Remove ads upgrade are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing a result

When you finish a puzzle you can choose to share a short text summary (mode, time, hints used, streak). It opens your device’s own share sheet and goes only where you send it — never to us — and never includes the puzzle grid.

Advertising

The free version offers optional short rewarded videos, served by Google AdMob, that you can choose to watch in exchange for extra hints. There are no banners and no ads between puzzles; a video only ever plays when you tap to start it.

Ads run in Google’s limited ads mode: not personalised, and with no advertising identifier used to profile you. We do not request ads based on your interests or a profile of you, anywhere in the world. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Because nothing is personalised and no advertising identifier is used to profile you, the app shows no advertising consent form and no tracking prompt. Two things do still happen, and we would rather state them plainly than leave them to be discovered: Google uses your device’s IP address to deliver the ad, and it may store a small amount of data on the device for the sole purpose of detecting invalid traffic and fraud. Neither is used to build a profile of you or to follow you across other apps or websites.

Purchasing the Remove ads upgrade gives unlimited hints without ads, so no ad is ever requested. You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier at any time in your device’s own settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). The app does not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals, because it is not a website and has no browser to receive them from.

Purchases

The one-time Remove ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Because we collect and hold no personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, or delete — but if you have any questions or concerns, we genuinely want to hear them. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
