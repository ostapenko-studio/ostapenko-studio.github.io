What the app is — and isn't

Mow Day turns third-party weather forecast data (WeatherAPI.com) into practical mowing advice: whether conditions look workable now, the next stretch of the day with room for the whole mow, and what would cut that stretch short. Everything it shows is a reading of a forecast, not a promise about the weather, and not a measurement of your lawn. The app cannot see how long your grass is, how soft the ground is underfoot, or exactly what fell on it before the forecast began; forecasts shift, showers arrive unannounced, and microclimates are real — a shaded lawn on heavy soil dries differently from an open one on sand. Your grass and the ground under it always get the final say, and decisions about mowing — including whether it is safe to operate your mower — are yours.
