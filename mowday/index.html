Mow Day

When can I mow the lawn — and does the whole job fit before the rain?

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

The app

What you get

  • A verdict in plain words — great time to mow, good time to mow, grass likely damp, rain close behind, or better to wait — with the reason in one sentence.
  • The next mowing window, down to the minute, sized to the mow you actually do: tell it how long a mow takes and which hours you are free, and it stops offering gaps you cannot use.
  • A start-by time, and what closes the door: rain, the light going, or the end of the hours you set.
  • Whether the grass has had a chance to dry off — after rain, and after a heavy dew — with an estimate of when it should.
  • The next three days as mowing advice, with the best day tagged.
  • Quiet, optional alerts: a heads-up the evening before a good mowing day, and a nudge when the window opens. Nothing else, ever.
  • Two things about your lawn that change the answer: shade, and ground that stays soft.

Honest about forecasts

A forecast is a guess, and Mow Day says so. It cannot see your lawn — not how long the grass is, not how soft the ground is underfoot, and not exactly what fell on it before the forecast began. When it cannot see far enough back, it tells you that too. The last look at the grass stays with you.

Quietly respectful

No accounts, no tracking, no lawn diary. Your location goes straight to the weather service to fetch the forecast and nowhere else — we run no servers and collect nothing.

Free with a single unobtrusive banner; one small one-time purchase removes it forever. No subscriptions, ever.
