CarWash Day

Is now a good time to wash the car — and is it worth it, given the weather afterwards?

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

The app

What you get

  • Two answers on one screen: whether the hour or two ahead suits the wash, and what the forecast shows once you have finished.
  • A wash window sized to the whole job, with the reason in one sentence — rain, sun full on the panels, wind, cold or daylight.
  • The outlook after the wash as two separate facts: the peak hourly chance of rain, and the forecast rainfall.
  • Three days of advice, with the day worth choosing marked — or the soonest workable one, if you are in a hurry.
  • Washing at home or driving to a car wash: the weather that matters differs, and the app knows which you do.
  • One optional heads-up, the evening before a day that looks good. Nothing else, ever.

Honest about forecasts

A forecast is a guess, and dry weather is not the same as a clean car. CarWash Day cannot see your car — not how dirty it is, not what is on the paint, not where it is parked — and when the forecast cannot see far enough ahead, it says so rather than assuming the best.

Whatever it says about rain, bird mess, tree sap and bugs are best rinsed off straight away.

Quietly respectful

No accounts, no tracking, no wash diary. Your location goes straight to the weather service to fetch the forecast and nowhere else — we run no servers and collect nothing.

Free with a single unobtrusive banner; one small one-time purchase removes it forever. No subscriptions, ever.
