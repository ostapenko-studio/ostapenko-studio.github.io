What the app is — and isn't

CarWash Day turns third-party weather forecast data (WeatherAPI.com) into practical advice about washing a car. It answers two separate questions: whether the hours ahead suit the wash itself, and what the weather looks like afterwards, once you have finished. Everything it shows is a reading of a forecast about the weather, and not a judgement about your car. The app cannot see how dirty the car is, what is already on the paint, where it is parked, or what your water restrictions allow. Forecasts shift, showers arrive unannounced, and microclimates are real — a car under a tree fares differently from one on an open driveway. Two things follow, and the app says both on screen: a dry forecast is not the same as a clean car, and contamination does not wait for good weather — bird mess, tree sap and the like are best rinsed off when you find them, whatever the forecast says. Decisions about washing your car are yours.
