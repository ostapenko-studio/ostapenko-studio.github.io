The app

What you get

  • One notification the evening before frost is likely — around 6 pm, in time to cover the tomatoes, never at dawn when it's too late.
  • An honest risk call, not a raw number: forecast low, clear skies, and still air are read together, the way frost actually forms.
  • The nights ahead at a glance, so a run of cold nights never surprises you.
  • A place is set once and watched all season. Multiple places — home, the garden, the block — each with its own threshold.
  • Off-season quiet: from October to April the app rests, and says so.

Built for Australia

Frost country here runs from the Southern Tablelands to the Mallee. Frost Alert speaks Australian: minus signs are rare, "cover the tomatoes" is not, and the season it watches is May to September.

Quietly respectful

No tracking. No data sales. Your coordinates go straight to the weather service to fetch the forecast and nowhere else — we run no servers and collect nothing.

Free with a single unobtrusive banner; one small one-time purchase removes it forever. No subscriptions, ever.
