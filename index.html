Meet the team

Ostapenko Studio is a two-person independent studio based in Brisbane, Australia. We are both graduates of Saint Petersburg State University, have worked together since 2013, and design, build and support every app here ourselves.

Yury Ostapenko

Co-founder · Engineering

A software engineer with more than twenty years’ experience building systems that people rely on every day. At the studio, Yury leads engineering: the data, the reliability and the behind-the-scenes work that keeps each app accurate and fast.

Iuliia Ostapenko

Co-founder · Interface design

A front-end engineer with more than fifteen years’ experience designing and building the interfaces people use every day. At the studio, Iuliia leads interface design and front-end development, keeping every app clear, consistent and easy to use.
